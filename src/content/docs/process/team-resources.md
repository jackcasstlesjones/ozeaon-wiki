The links the team opens every day, in one place.

## Figma

[OZEAON DESIGN 2.0](https://www.figma.com/design/Y7P66cqHVcJYOw83eqAk1m/OZEAON-DESIGN-2.0) is the source of truth for everything visual. Specs never restate what Figma shows - see [Documentation Conventions](/ozeaon-wiki/process/documentation-conventions/). Once a ticket reaches Design Done the developer-facing Figma is frozen, and changes go through a [change request](/ozeaon-wiki/process/change-requests/).

## Monday

- [OZEAON Tasks board](https://ozeaon.monday.com/boards/5015597431/views/36242636) - the 11-column board every ticket moves through, from Specs Pending to Done. See [Kanban](/ozeaon-wiki/process/kanban/).
- [OZEAON Bugs board](https://ozeaon.monday.com/boards/5015597434/views/39519321) - the bug queue. Bugs reach it through triage: external reports come in via the [bug report form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc3XCR9CQah3jCu-Uw6a-htuVCTXGMmMmYtrG9QQacyI_EhDQ/viewform), internal finds go to BA/PM. See [Change Requests](/ozeaon-wiki/process/change-requests/).

## Google Drive

The [team shared drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/0AMh0RaOrsSQgUk9PVA) holds the team's shared files. If the link opens a "request access" page, switch to your OZEAON Google account with the account picker.
