## What it involves (5 deliverables)

- Report action on articles, projects, comments and profiles
- Report reason taxonomy and duplicate handling
- Moderation queue with triage states and an action log
- Outcome notice to reporter and author (DSA statement of reasons)
- Appeal route against a removal
