## What it involves (8 deliverables)

- Automated and manual audit of shipped surfaces
- Keyboard navigation and focus management
- Screen-reader semantics and landmark structure
- Colour contrast, including the category colour system
- Form labelling and error messaging (ties into Forms rework)
- Media captions, transcripts and alt-text authoring flow
- Published accessibility statement
- Regression checks in CI so the fixes hold
