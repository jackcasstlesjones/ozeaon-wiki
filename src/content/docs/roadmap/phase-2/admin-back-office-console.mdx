## What it involves (9 deliverables)

- User and organisation administration
- Role-based admin permissions and least-privilege access
- Moderation queue surfaced in the console (built in Content reporting)
- Payment operations: refunds, disputes, payout investigation
- Entitlement and feature-flag management
- Curation tooling for Featured and the Educational Resources library
- Manual adjustment surface for balances and grants
- Audit log of every admin action
- Support view onto a single user's account state
