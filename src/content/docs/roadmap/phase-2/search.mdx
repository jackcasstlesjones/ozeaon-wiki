## What it involves (6 deliverables)

- Search infrastructure choice and index pipeline
- Indexing for articles, projects, users, organisations
- Re-index hooks for content types shipping later (Educational Resources, Open Calls, Events)
- Query parsing, ranking and typo tolerance
- Filters, facets and result-type tabs
- Permission-aware results (drafts, paywalled, protocol-gated content)
