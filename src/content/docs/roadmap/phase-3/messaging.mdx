## What it involves (7 deliverables)

- Conversation and message data model
- Realtime transport and delivery guarantees
- Read receipts, typing state and unread counts
- Blocking and privacy enforcement (depends on Connections & blocking)
- Attachments and link handling
- Abuse reporting and moderation surface for DMs
- Retention and export obligations
