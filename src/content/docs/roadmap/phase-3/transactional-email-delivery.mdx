## What it involves (7 deliverables)

- Provider selection and sending-domain authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
- Template system, branding and plain-text fallbacks
- Per-type routing, unsubscribe handling and a preference centre
- Bounce, complaint and suppression-list handling
- Deliverability monitoring and reputation warm-up
- Transactional vs marketing separation and consent records
- Localisation hooks for translated templates
