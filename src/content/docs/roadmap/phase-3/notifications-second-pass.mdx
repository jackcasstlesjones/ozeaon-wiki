## What it involves (11 deliverables)

- Notification settings screen with per-type preferences and mute controls
- Quick actions — accept or decline straight from the notification
- Grouping and digest rollups for repeat events on one object
- Fan-out and batching so high-volume actors do not flood feeds
- Retention and automatic clearing (the 30-day and one-year rules)
- Scheduled, time-based notifications (“ends in three days”)
- Payment, payout and refund notification types
- Funding round and contribution types
- Review, bounty and Open Call application types
- Role change types
- Email digest rollups across the new types
