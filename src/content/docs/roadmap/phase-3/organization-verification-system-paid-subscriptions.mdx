## What it involves (5 deliverables)

- Verification application, review and badge granting
- Subscription plans and pricing tiers for organisations
- Recurring billing on Stripe: checkout, renewals, failed payments and cancellation
- Entitlement gating so paid-tier features follow the subscription state
- Billing surface in organisation settings with invoices
