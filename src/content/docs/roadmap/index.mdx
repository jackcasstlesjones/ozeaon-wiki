Everything OZEAON is building, in priority order. Each of the 63 items has its own page with its effort size and, where it has been scoped, a breakdown of what it involves.

<div class="roadmap-stats">
<div><strong>63</strong><span>features</span></div>
<div><strong>243</strong><span>scoped deliverables</span></div>
<div><strong>17</strong><span>major initiatives (XL and XXL)</span></div>
<div><strong>5</strong><span>stages, ending in a token economy</span></div>
</div>

9 items have shipped and 3 are in progress. 51 are still to build. 10 are compelled by law and 16 are backend work.

## Effort scale

| Size | Meaning | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| S | Quick win | 2 weeks |
| M | Moderate | 3 weeks |
| L | Substantial | 6 weeks |
| XL | Major initiative | 12 weeks |
| XXL | Longer-timeline initiative | 20 weeks |

Flagged **Legal**: compelled by statute. The timing is a legal exposure question, not a prioritisation one.
