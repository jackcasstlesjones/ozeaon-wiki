## What it involves (11 deliverables)

- Balance and earning-event data model
- Earning hooks: publishing, peer review, Open Calls, funding, tipping
- Emissions adjustment ladder (bidirectional, treasury-share driven)
- Inactivity decay tiers and Foundation Treasury recycling
- Activity buffs and streak multipliers with emission caps
- Attestor / signed-claim pathway for variable rewards
- Treasury accounting and reconciliation reporting
- Backfill of pre-ledger tipping and funding events (pending decision)
- Anti-farming limits and audit trail
- Founding-member 1,000 PRG grant, keyed off the alpha badge
- Reconciliation against the white paper $OZN and PRG supply tables
