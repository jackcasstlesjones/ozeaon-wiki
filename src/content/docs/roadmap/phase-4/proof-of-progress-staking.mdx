## What it involves (7 deliverables)

- Staking rounds, minimum 50 PRG / full probability at 100 PRG
- Verifiable randomness integration (Chainlink VRF or equivalent)
- Consecutive-loss boost at 314 basis points per loss
- Minting from the 750M $OZN reserve with reserve exhaustion handling
- PRG cost on win routed to Foundation Treasury
- Auto-unstake below threshold
- Hardening: the off-chain ledger becomes the minting authority for a liquid asset
