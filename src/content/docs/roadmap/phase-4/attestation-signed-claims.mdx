## What it involves (7 deliverables)

- Attestor roles, authorisation and governance control of the attestor set
- Claim schema, signing and verification
- Claim issuance surfaces for reviewers, sponsors, verifiers and pods
- Replay protection and single-use enforcement
- Revocation and downstream clawback
- Audit trail of every issued and revoked claim
- On-chain attestation path once the wallet layer exists
