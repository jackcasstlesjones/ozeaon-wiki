## What it involves (7 deliverables)

- Proposal deposits by actor type (100 / 200 / 300 PRG)
- Per-actor vote caps
- Tiered refund ladder on a 72-hour window
- 85% passing threshold and quorum handling
- Proposal timing windows up to 30 days
- Deposit forfeiture routing to Foundation Treasury
- Overlap to resolve with DAO pods
