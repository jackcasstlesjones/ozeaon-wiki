## What it involves (8 deliverables)

- Foundation PRG treasury balances, inflows and recycling
- Off-chain treasury ledger and reconciliation reporting
- Disbursement request, approval and execution workflow
- Pod, organisation and project group treasuries (white paper 5.12)
- Budget allocation, spend caps and delegated authority limits
- On-chain multisig binding once the wallet layer lands
- DAO treasury disbursement fee (0-1%, governance-configurable)
- Public treasury reporting
