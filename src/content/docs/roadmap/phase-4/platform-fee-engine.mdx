## What it involves (5 deliverables)

- Governance-configurable rates with bounded min / max
- Fee bands: funding 5%, tipping 9%, bounties 9%
- Migration from hard-coded Phase 1 Stripe fee take
- DAO treasury disbursement fee (0-1%, optional)
- Fee revenue reporting and Foundation Reserve routing
