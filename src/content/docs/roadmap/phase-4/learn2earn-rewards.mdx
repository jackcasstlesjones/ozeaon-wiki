## What it involves (6 deliverables)

- 1 PRG per correct answer, +1 PRG to resource creator
- Answer-verification hardening against client-side tampering
- Per-user, per-resource and per-period earning caps
- Retake and repeat-answer rules
- Streak multiplier integration
- Creator earnings visibility
