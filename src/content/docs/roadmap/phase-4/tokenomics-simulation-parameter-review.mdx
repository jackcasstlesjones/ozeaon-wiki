## What it involves (7 deliverables)

- Model PRG emissions ladder against projected activity
- Model $OZN mint rate from Proof-of-Progress under realistic PRG supply
- Close the self-dealing tipping loop (10 PRG per US$1 vs 50 PRG per $OZN)
- Sybil-resistance requirements and cost-per-fake-account analysis
- Activity buff caps and runaway-emission bounds
- Inactivity decay and treasury recycling behaviour
- Publish revised parameter table and reconcile against the white paper
