## What it involves (7 deliverables)

- Custodial vs self-custody implementation (set by the regulatory assessment)
- Wallet provisioning and account binding
- Key management, rotation and recovery flows
- Gas abstraction / sponsored transactions
- Transaction, pending and failure states across every money surface
- Chain and RPC provider selection with failover
- Security review of the key-handling path
