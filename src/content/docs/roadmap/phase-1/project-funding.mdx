## What it involves (7 deliverables)

- Funding goals, progress display and contribution records
- One-off and recurring contribution flows
- Payout release to project owners via Connect
- Refund policy and failed-goal handling
- Funder-facing receipts and contribution history
- Public funder list with anonymity option
- Event retention for later PRG backfill (see retroactivity decision)
