## What it involves (6 deliverables)

- Per-article pay-gating toggle for authors
- Price setting and currency rules
- Purchase flow, entitlement storage and access checks
- Preview / teaser rendering for non-purchasers
- Author earnings view and payout routing
- Fee take at 9%
