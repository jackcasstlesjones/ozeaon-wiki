## What it involves (9 deliverables)

- Stripe account, webhook ingestion and idempotent event handling
- Customer and payment-method storage, SCA / 3DS flows
- Stripe Connect onboarding for payout recipients (KYC, verification states)
- Payouts, balance handling and payout scheduling
- Refunds, chargebacks and dispute handling
- Platform fee take at source (feeds the Phase 4 fee engine)
- Tax reporting surface and invoice / receipt generation
- Currency handling and minimum-amount rules
- Founding-member platform-fee exemption, keyed off the alpha badge
